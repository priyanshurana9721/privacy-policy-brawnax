13. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:

Data Deletion Requests:
You maintain full ownership rights over your personal profile data. You can issue a secure data removal request at any time by contacting our support team directly at support@brawnax.com or using the processing systems within your profile dashboard inside the application.
